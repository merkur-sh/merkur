Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

1. Who we are

Merkur is a personal project of Dmytro Pletenskyi, a private individual who controls your personal data. Contact: dmitriy.pletenskoy@gmail.com. Postal address: see our Contact / Impressum page. No data protection officer is required or appointed.

2. What we never see

Your password (sign-in uses OPAQUE, so it never leaves your browser), the contents of your terminal (encrypted end to end between your browser and your machine) and your private keys. Our relay sees only which account and machine a connection belongs to, and the size and timing of encrypted traffic.

4. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. The app stores your settings and keys in your browser because it needs them to work. Nothing is used for tracking or advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Our providers are Railway (Netherlands), Fly.io, Hetzner and Axiom (Germany), Resend (Ireland), Rybbit (EU) and GitHub (United States). Providers that process data on our behalf are bound by data processing agreements; GitHub receives download requests from the daemon under its own privacy terms. If you turn on notifications, they are delivered through your browser vendor's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla), and their content is encrypted. Transfers outside the EEA, including to the US, and access from outside the EEA are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which are published by the European Commission and the US Department of Commerce.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or send marketing emails. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and linked machines: while your account exists; erased 7 days after you delete it.
  • Inactive accounts with email sign-in: deleted after 12 months without sign-in and 30 days' email notice. Signing in cancels deletion. Username-only accounts are not deleted for inactivity because they have no verified email address.
  • Browser sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out or revoke them.
  • Verification codes: 10 minutes. Sign-in attempt counters: 1 hour.
  • Push endpoint: until you turn off notifications or delete your account.
  • Logs, relay connection data and performance reports: 30 days.
  • Website analytics: visits cannot be linked to you after the day they occur; aggregated statistics are kept only while useful for understanding website use.
  • Waitlist email: until we send that one email, you withdraw consent, or 12 months pass. A daily database job removes expired addresses.
  • Abuse reports: 12 months; suspended accounts, including their emails: deleted 24 months after suspension. Database retention runs daily; hosted box deletion is retried until the host confirms removal.
  • If the service shuts down: all personal data is deleted within 30 days of shutdown, unless the law requires otherwise.

Waitlist expiry, suspended-account retention and account inactivity are checked daily. Deletion is processed in the next daily cleanup after the applicable period ends.

We keep data longer only where the law requires it or to deal with a security incident or legal claim.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Delete your account under Settings, Account; for anything else, email us. We reply within one month, free of charge. You may complain to a data protection authority in the EU country where you live, work or where the issue occurred, or in Ukraine to the Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

9. Your right to object

You may object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling grounds or need the data for legal claims.

10. Security

We use end-to-end encryption, OPAQUE sign-in and signed releases (details). No system is fully secure. We will report any data breach to the authorities and, where the risk to you is high, to you.

11. Children

The service is not for anyone under 18. If we learn we hold a child's data, we delete it.

12. Transfer of the service

If the service passes to a new operator, we will tell you who it is at least 30 days in advance, and you may delete your account first. The new operator will become responsible for your data.

13. Changes

We will post changes here with a new date and notify you of material changes in advance. Questions: dmitriy.pletenskoy@gmail.com.

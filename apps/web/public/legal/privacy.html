3. Cookies and browser storage

The app uses a cookie to keep you signed in, and stores your settings, keys and a cache of recent terminal screens in your browser. These are strictly necessary for the service you request.

4. Recipients and transfers

We use these providers: Hetzner (servers, database, relay and website analytics, in Germany), Axiom (logs and performance reports, stored in the EU), Resend (email), Cloudflare (website traffic, not the app, and encrypted backups stored in the EU) and GitHub (daemon downloads and updates). If you turn on notifications, your browser vendor's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers them. Providers that process data on our behalf do so under data processing terms; GitHub and push services handle requests under their own privacy terms. We disclose data to authorities where the law requires it.

Some providers are based in, or may access data from, countries outside the EEA, including the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, published by the US Department of Commerce and the European Commission.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and linked machines: while your account exists, and erased about 7 days after you ask to delete it; signing in before then cancels deletion.
  • Accounts with email sign-in: may be deleted after about 12 months without sign-in, following notice by email. Username-only accounts have no verified email address and are not currently deleted for inactivity.
  • Browser sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out or revoke them.
  • Verification codes and attempt counters: from minutes up to about a day.
  • Hosted boxes and their contents: until you delete the box or your account, or a password reset deletes it.
  • Push endpoint: until you turn off notifications or delete your account.
  • Logs, relay connection data and performance reports: up to about 30 days.
  • Website analytics: while useful for understanding how the website is used.
  • Waitlist email: until we send that email, you withdraw consent, or about 12 months pass.
  • Abuse reports: about 12 months. Suspended accounts, including their email addresses: about 24 months after suspension.
  • Backups: up to about five weeks, so deleted data can remain in a backup for that long.

We may keep data longer where the law requires it or to deal with a security incident or a legal claim.

6. Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your data. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. Delete your account under Settings, Account; for anything else, email us. We reply within the time the law sets. You may complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, work or where the issue occurred, or in Ukraine to the Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

7. Your right to object

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will then stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims.

8. Changes

If the service passes to another operator, such as a company we set up, that operator becomes responsible for your data; we will tell you where the law requires.

We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version. Where the law requires, we will tell you about changes. Questions: dmitriy.pletenskoy@gmail.com.

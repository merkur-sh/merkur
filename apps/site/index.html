Questions

What do I install?+

A small daemon on each Mac or Linux machine you want to reach. It dials out to Merkur, so there is no SSH, no open port and no VPN. Link it once, then open it from any browser.

Which browsers work?+

Chrome and Edge 113+, Safari 26.4+ on macOS, iOS and iPadOS, Firefox 141+ on Windows and 145+ on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 26, and Chrome 121+ on Android. The daemon runs on macOS 13+ and Linux.

Can Merkur see my terminal?+

No. The server signs you in, keeps your machine list and issues short-lived session tickets. It is never in the path of a keystroke. Sessions are sealed end to end with ML-KEM-1024 and Noise.

What if my network blocks the direct connection?+

Your browser connects straight to your machine over WebTransport. When a network blocks that, a relay carries the session instead. It forwards sealed frames and cannot read them.

Does it work on a phone?+

Yes. Merkur adds its own key row above the keyboard: Esc, Ctrl, Alt, Paste, PgUp, PgDn and Tab. Add the site to your home screen and a terminal bell can arrive as a notification.

What does it cost?+

It is free during the beta for up to [[limit.value]] linked machines. The free beta is temporary; we will tell you at least 14 days before it ends. See the Terms.
